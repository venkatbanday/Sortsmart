Typical today

  1. Unload bags to feeder
  2. Sort by product type
  3. Scan, then move to an empty cage
  4. Walk to find the cage that matches the postcode
  5. Scan and move again

Proposed

  1. Pre-bag by product or area at the origin
  2. Ramp and conveyor to speed up unloading
  3. One scan, cage number shown straight away
  4. Lane serials match postcode order
  5. Fewer moves and fewer double operations
